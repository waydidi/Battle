## Risks to keep in view

Solo-creator scope · asset licensing · server bills before measured load · economy exploits ·
content growth · mobile as a separate effort · AI kept out of live rules · PvP fairness and
moderation · ongoing live-ops cost. Use placeholder art, track costs by category, and do not
rent large infrastructure before the vertical slice works.
